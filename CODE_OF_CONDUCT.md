# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory jokes or imagery, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through GitHub's private security-advisory reporting flow or to repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may take any action they deem appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
